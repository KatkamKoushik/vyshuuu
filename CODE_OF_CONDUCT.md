# VYSHUUU // Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the **VYSHUUU** project a respectful, harassment-free, and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or technical experience.

## Standards

Positive behaviors that contribute to a healthy project environment include:
* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints, artistic interpretations, and architectural ideas
* Gracefully accepting constructive feedback on creative and technical implementations
* Prioritizing the preservation and elevation of Indian handloom textile heritage
* Maintaining high engineering rigor across accessibility, visual fidelity, and performance

Unacceptable behaviors include:
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks
* Public or private harassment of any kind
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional development setting

## Responsibilities & Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

## Contact & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainer at `koushikkatkam@gmail.com`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
